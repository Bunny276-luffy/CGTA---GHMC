# GHMC Boundary Dataset Provenance

- **Source Authority**: Telangana State Remote Sensing Applications Centre (TGRAC), Government of Telangana
- **Source URL**: `https://tgrac.telangana.gov.in/arcgis/rest/services/TIS_Folder/HMDA_ORR_GHMC_Water_Bodies/MapServer/6`
- **Layer**: HMDA ORR GHMC Boundary (Layer 6)
- **Filter Query**: `Admin = 'GHMC'`
- **Coordinate Reference System (CRS)**: WGS84 / EPSG:4326 (expressed in decimal degree longitude/latitude coordinates)
- **Retrieval Date**: August 15, 2026
- **Description**: Authorized spatial boundary coordinates of the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) administrative jurisdiction limits, containing 8 exterior rings defining the main service boundaries and islands/exclaves.
